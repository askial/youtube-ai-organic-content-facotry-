# Story formulas

## Reference teardown (the reel this repo was built from)
- 66 s total: ~42 s wordless story + ~24 s talking-head CTA (we skip the CTA).
- ~14–16 shots, avg ~3 s each; 9:16; stylized 3D animated look; rainy city at dusk.
- Structure: a kid gives his money to a man begging → instead buys a box of
  umbrellas → sets the man up selling them in the rain → the man earns enough → time jump
  → the man owns a bright umbrella shop → the now-grown kid passes by → recognition, hug,
  a few spoken lines with word-by-word captions.
- What makes it work: an instantly readable need (cardboard sign), a symbolic object
  (umbrella) in every act, a warm/cool colour shift from setup (blue rain) to payoff
  (warm shop light), a time jump, and a reunion where the gift is returned or acknowledged.

## Formulas (rotate these)
1. **Kindness Repaid** — A helps B with a small, clever gift (not just cash) → time jump
   → B is thriving and repays/acknowledges A.
2. **Teach, Don't Give** — instead of a handout, A gives B a means to earn (tools,
   a skill). Payoff: B now teaches someone else (the chain continues).
3. **The Keeper** — A keeps an object safe for someone who never came back → years later
   the owner's child returns for it.
4. **Underdog Workshop** — a kid fails publicly → an old neighbour quietly helps
   them practise → payoff: success, and the kid finds the neighbour in the front row.
5. **Loyal Companion** — a pet waits at the same spot every day → time jump → reunion.
6. **Pay It Forward Loop** — a kindness passes through 3–4 strangers and returns to the
   original giver in the last shot (circular structure).
7. **Hidden Sacrifice** — we see a character seemingly being selfish; the reveal shows
   why (they were saving up for someone else).

## Hook bank (first 2 seconds)
- A hand-written sign ("ANYTHING HELPS", "LOST DOG", "CLOSED FOREVER").
- A child's face pressed to a shop window.
- An object breaking/being dropped in slow motion.
- Two contrasting characters in one frame (rich/poor, young/old, tall/small).
